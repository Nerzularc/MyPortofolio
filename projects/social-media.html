Brand analysis

Fictional brand. Arunika Coffee Roasters, its handle and its products are invented for this exercise. All metrics on this page are simulated to show how I would track and report. They are not real results.

PositioningTraceable Indonesian single-origin coffee, roasted to order and shipped to coffee lovers in Singapore and Australia.
AudienceHome brewers aged 24–40 who own a pour-over or AeroPress, value origin stories and buy online.
VoiceWarm, knowledgeable, never snobby. It explains, it doesn't lecture. Short sentences, one emoji at most per paragraph.
Competitive landscape (types)Local roasters in SG and AU with strong community; large subscription brands with big ad budgets; marketplace sellers competing on price.
Differentiator to amplifyOrigin access: grower stories from Gayo, Toraja and Flores that other brands can't easily copy.
Month goalGrow saves and shares (signals of useful content) and drive clicks to the holiday gift box.

Performance tracking dashboard (simulated metrics)

Engagement rate = (likes + comments + shares + saves) ÷ reach. Reach-based rates are fairer than follower-based ones when Reels reach non-followers.

Engagement analysis

    Caveat: 15 posts are too few to draw strong conclusions. In a real account I would compare against the brand's own 90-day baseline and run at least 3 posts per format before changing the mix.

    Monthly reporting template

    MONTHLY SOCIAL REPORT — [Brand] — [Month]
1. Headline: one sentence on whether we hit the month goal
2. KPIs vs last month: Reach · Avg engagement rate · Saves · Link clicks · Follower change
3. Top 3 posts (why they worked) / Bottom 3 posts (what we'll change)
4. Pillar performance table + recommended mix for next month
5. Community: notable comments, UGC received, questions to answer in content
6. Experiments run → result → keep / drop
7. Next month: 3 priorities, content needs from the client (photos, product info), approvals pending
Data source: platform insights exported [date]; metrics definitions on page 2
