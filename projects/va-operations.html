Daily task management dashboard

Simulated week. These 20 tasks show how I log work, priority, time and AI use for a client. They are not records of real client work.

Priority definitions: P1 means today or it blocks someone. P2 means this week. P3 means when capacity allows. Time is logged in minutes to support honest invoicing.

Email management SOP (summary)

  1. Two triage passes a day at the start and end of my shift. The founder sees a summary, not the raw inbox.
  2. Label system: 1-Action-Founder, 2-Action-VA, 3-Waiting, 4-Read-Later and 5-Receipts. Newsletters are filtered automatically.
  3. Two-minute rule: anything I can answer from approved templates, I reply to and log.
  4. Drafts, not sends, for anything new, sensitive, financial or legal. Those go to the founder for approval.
  5. Response targets: clients within 1 business day, vendors within 2.
  6. End-of-day note: three lines covering what was handled, what needs the founder and what is waiting on others.

Calendar management workflow

  1. Protect focus blocks first: the founder's deep-work hours are marked busy.
  2. Check time zones for every external invite. Write both times in the invite title, for example "16:00 WIB / 09:00 GMT".
  3. Leave a 15-minute buffer between back-to-back calls, and allow no more than 4 hours of meetings a day unless approved.
  4. Every meeting gets an agenda link using the meeting preparation template. No agenda after 24 hours means the meeting is flagged.
  5. Confirm the day before for external meetings, and keep a reschedule log.
  6. Resolve conflicts with a rule: clients come before investors, then team, then vendors, unless the founder says otherwise.

Google Sheets templates

These are delivered as one Excel workbook that opens directly in Google Sheets, with formulas that recalculate.

  • P4 Task Tracker: the 20-task log with variance (actual − estimate) and a status summary using COUNTIF.
  • P4 Weekly Report: totals by category using SUMIF, the completion rate and the AI-assisted share.
  • P2 Leads CRM and P5 Social Metrics follow the same conventions: inputs in blue, formulas in black.

Practical AI workflows (ChatGPT and Claude)

Each workflow has a human checkpoint. I never paste confidential client data into an AI tool unless the client approves that tool.

WorkflowPrompt patternHuman check before sending
Inbox summary"Summarise these 15 email subjects and snippets into: needs founder / I can handle / FYI. Do not invent details."Open every "needs founder" email to confirm
Reply drafting"Draft a reply in [founder]'s tone (examples below). Max 120 words. Leave [brackets] for anything you don't know."Fill brackets, check names, dates and amounts
Meeting minutes"From these notes, extract decisions and action items as a table with owner and due date. Mark missing owners as UNASSIGNED."Compare against the notes and confirm owners
Research summary"Summarise the attached sources only. For each claim, give the source number. Say 'not in sources' if unsure."Click every source and verify each number
Weekly report"Turn this task log into the weekly report template. Keep numbers exactly as given."Recalculate totals in the sheet
