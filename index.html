How I work

I follow the same five steps whether the task is a lead list, a research brief or an inbox. AI speeds up the middle steps, and I own every check.

  1. Clarify the brief

    Confirm goal, audience, format, deadline and what "done" means. Ask questions in one message, not five.

  2. Collect from sources

    Primary sources first: official sites, filings, pricing pages. Log URL and access date for every data point.

  3. Structure the data

    Turn notes into consistent fields, controlled values and a sheet that can be filtered, scored and reused.

  4. Assist with AI, then verify

    Use AI for drafts, summaries and formatting. Then fact-check, spot-check 10% of rows, and fix what fails.

  5. Deliver and document

    Send the output with a short summary, open questions and an SOP, so the work can be repeated.

No invented data

If I can't verify it, I label it as an estimate or leave it blank. Synthetic or simulated data is always marked.

Client data stays private

I don't paste confidential client information into AI tools without permission, and I follow the client's tool policy.

Written, async by default

End-of-day updates, clear file naming and decision logs, so a client in another time zone never waits on me.
