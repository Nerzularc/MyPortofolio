Overview (general profile)

The first two lines show in search results, so they say what you get.

I turn messy research and repetitive admin into clean, structured, verified work: lead lists with sources, research briefs with citations, and inboxes and calendars that run on clear SOPs.

What I can help with • B2B prospect research: ICP-based company lists, decision-maker titles, scoring, CRM-ready sheets (HubSpot, Google Sheets, Excel) • Market and competitor research: pricing comparisons, SWOT, cited summaries with every source dated • AI output evaluation: rubric scoring, hallucination checks, instruction-following checks, written rationales • Virtual assistance: inbox triage, calendar management across time zones, meeting notes, weekly reports

How I work

  1. I confirm the goal, format and definition of "done" before starting.
  2. I log the source for every data point.
  3. I use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini) for speed, and I verify every output before delivery.
  4. You get the deliverable, a short summary and any open questions in one message.
