**Reviewer stance:** a strict international recruiter screening for junior remote roles (AI evaluator, research or lead-gen assistant, VA or operations assistant). Scores run from 1 to 10. A score of 7+ means "competitive for junior remote roles"; 9+ needs paid client evidence, which no portfolio can manufacture.

## 2. Weaknesses found and revised in this build

1. **Position bias in the AI evaluation data.** On the first pass, the better response was always in slot A, which an AI-training recruiter would spot immediately. *Fixed:* the better answer is now in slot B for 10 of 24 records (A 14, B 9, Tie 1).
2. **Unrealistically perfect winners.** Every preferred response scored 5/5 on every dimension. *Fixed:* five preferred responses now have documented minor flaws, with rationales.
3. **Lead tiers too generous.** On the first pass, 66% of synthetic leads were "qualified", which is implausible. *Fixed:* thresholds tightened to 78/62/42, giving 12 Tier A, 42 Tier B, 38 Nurture and 8 Disqualified.
4. **Inconsistent social data.** Calendar formats and AI-use labels didn't match the written posts. *Fixed:* the calendar is now derived from the posts.
5. **Over-claimed insights.** The engagement analysis stated a pillar strategy that the simulated data didn't support. *Fixed:* insights are computed from the data, worded neutrally and carry a small-sample caveat.
6. **Factual slip on the homepage.** It said "six research firms" where the report has seven estimates. *Fixed.*
7. **Report and data mismatch** in the VA weekly report risk line. *Fixed.*
8. **Rendering bugs:** bar charts invisible, mobile horizontal overflow, long table rows. *Fixed and re-checked at 390 px and 1366 px.*
9. **Unverifiable legal claims.** Compliance notes had no sources. *Fixed:* ICO/PECR, the Spam Act (ALRC, legislation.gov.au), IMDA/Spam Control Act and FTC/CAN-SPAM sources added.

## 4. Verdict

> **Shortlist for junior remote roles.** As a first-year student, the candidate shows unusual initiative: a founded community, organisational roles and national-level awards. The portfolio is honest, structured and directly relevant. It demonstrates the habits that matter in remote work: written process, source logging, QA and responsible AI use. To move from "promising" to "hire", the candidate needs one real-data deliverable and one paid task.
