# 30-Day Portfolio & Job Application Roadmap

**Time budget:** 2–3 hours a day. **Goal by day 30:** portfolio live, 3 profiles complete, 40+ targeted applications, at least 3 interviews or test tasks, and the first paid task if possible.

---

## Week 1 (days 1–7): publish and personalise

| Day | Task | Done when |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Check the profile data now in the site and CVs (done on 19 Nov). Optional: take a free English test (for example EF SET) and add the score to your CVs. | Everything is correct |
| 2 | Create a GitHub repo, upload the folder, enable **Settings → Pages → Deploy from branch (main, root)**. | Site loads at your github.io URL |
| 3 | Read every case study and re-do 3 AI-evaluation records yourself without looking. Edit any wording that doesn't sound like you. | You can explain every number |
| 4 | Build a real Zapier free-tier Zap (form → sheet) and take screenshots for Project 4. | Screenshot added to `assets/img/` |
| 5 | Update LinkedIn (headline, About, Featured, Projects) from the kit. | Profile at "All-star" level |
| 6 | Create your Upwork profile and one specialised profile. | Profile submitted |
| 7 | Rest and review. Ask one friend or lecturer to proofread the site. | Feedback list |

## Week 2 (days 8–14): add one real-data project and start applying

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 8–10 | **Real-data mini project:** run the Project 2 SOP on 20 real companies, company-level public data only. Log the source URL for every field and don't collect personal emails. Publish it as "Project 2b: real-data sample". |
| 11 | Shortlist 20 jobs: AI evaluation and annotation, research assistant, lead gen, VA. Check that each accepts applicants from Indonesia. |
| 12–14 | Send 15 tailored applications using the proposal templates and the matching CV. Log each one in a tracker (date, role, link, CV version, status). |

## Week 3 (days 15–21): AI evaluation track and volume

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 15 | Apply to AI-training and annotation platforms that accept Indonesia. Read recent reviews about payment reliability first. |
| 16–17 | Complete any qualification assessments. Before each, re-read the rubric and Section F of the interview prep. |
| 18–20 | 15 more applications. Send one LinkedIn post (from the posting plan). |
| 21 | Review the tracker: which CV version and which role type got responses? Double down on that one. |

## Week 4 (days 22–30): convert and improve

| Day | Task |
|---|---|
| 22–24 | Follow up on applications older than 5 days. Practise interview answers out loud (record yourself once). |
| 25–27 | 10 more applications. Offer a small paid test task in every proposal. |
| 28 | Add any real feedback or results to the portfolio. Only add real results, and only with client permission. |
| 29 | Update the recruiter self-review scores and fix the weakest category. |
| 30 | Plan month 2: keep the role type with the best response rate and set the next 30-day targets. |

## Weekly targets

| Metric | Week 1 | Week 2 | Week 3 | Week 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Applications sent | 0 | 15 | 15 | 10 + follow-ups |
| Profile and portfolio updates | 5 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| Interviews or tests | – | 0–1 | 1–2 | 1–2 |

## Safety checklist (remote job scams)

- Never pay to apply, to "unlock" a job or for training equipment.
- Be careful with any chat-only "recruiter" who moves you to Telegram or WhatsApp fast.
- Share ID documents only through an official platform or a verified company HR system.
- Keep payment on-platform (for example Upwork) until you trust the client.
