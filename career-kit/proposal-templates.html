Rules for every proposal: (1) mention one specific detail from the job post in the first sentence; (2) link the most relevant case study, not the whole portfolio; (3) keep it under 150 words; (4) end with a simple question; (5) never claim experience you don't have.

1. AI evaluator / AI trainer application

Hi [Name / Hiring team],

Your post mentions [evaluating model responses for accuracy / following detailed guidelines]. That's the exact workflow I've practised and documented.

I built a five-dimension rubric and applied it to 24 side-by-side response pairs. I flagged fabricated citations, invented statistics and broken constraints, and wrote a short rationale for every decision: [link to AI evaluation case study].

I'm a Digital Innovation student at ITS (Surabaya, Indonesia), and I've completed IBM SkillsBuild courses on AI agents and on classifying data with IBM Granite. I write clearly in English and I'm careful with facts. I'm available [2–4] hours a day and comfortable with qualification tests.

Is there a sample task or assessment I can complete?

Nuzul

2. B2B lead generation / prospect list

Hi [Name],

You need [X] leads in [industry/region]. Before I start I'd confirm three things: your ideal company size, the job titles you sell to, and which signals matter (hiring, funding, tech stack).

Here's how I structure lead work: an ICP, a transparent scoring model, a source URL for every row, and verification only for qualified leads: [link to lead-gen case study]. (That demo uses synthetic data; your list would use real public sources.)

I can deliver a 20-row sample first so you can check quality before the full list.

Would a sample of 20 rows within [48 hours] work for you?

Nuzul

3. Virtual assistant / operations assistant

Hi [Name],

I noticed you need help with [inbox / calendar / reporting] while working across [time zones]. I'm in UTC+7, so I can clear the inbox and prepare your day before your morning starts.

I run admin through simple systems: label-based inbox triage, meeting briefs and minutes, and a Friday report. You can see the full setup, including SOPs and templates, here: [link to VA case study].

I use AI tools to draft faster, but nothing goes out without my check, and I only use tools you approve for your data.

What does a typical week of tasks look like for this role?

Nuzul

4. Market research assistant

Hi [Name],

For [the research question in the post], I'd start by agreeing the definition of the market. Published market sizes can differ by 4× depending on scope, as I found in my research on automation platforms: [link].

Every figure I deliver comes with its source, its date and a label (fact, estimate, assumption or hypothesis), so you know what you can rely on.

Which decision will this research support? That tells me what level of detail is worth paying for.

Nuzul

5. Short LinkedIn / email cold message to a potential client

Hi [Name], I saw [Company] is hiring for [ops/admin role]. While that role is open, I help teams with research, CRM upkeep and weekly reporting, 2–4 hours a day from UTC+7. Here's how I work: [portfolio link]. Would a small paid trial task be useful?

6. Follow-up (5–7 days later)

Hi [Name], just following up on my application for [role]. I'm happy to complete a short test task if that would help your decision. Thank you for considering me.
