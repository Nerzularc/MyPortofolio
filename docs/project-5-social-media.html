5. Canva-ready design instructions (all posts)

  1. Brand kit: espresso #3B2418, leaf #2F5D50, roast #A0522D, deep blue #1F4E79, white text. Add them under Canva → Brand Kit.
  2. Sizes: feed 1080×1350 (4:5); Reels and Stories 1080×1920 (9:16). Keep key text in the central safe area.
  3. Type: one bold sans for headlines (≥ 48 px), one regular sans for body (≥ 28 px). Two fonts maximum.
  4. Layout: headline in the bottom third, small wordmark at the bottom, 64 px margins.
  5. Templates: one master per format (carousel, single image, Reel cover, Story), duplicated for each post.
  6. Images: use the client's own photos. Disclose any AI-generated element in the brief, and never depict real people or places as if real.
  7. Export and naming: PNG (single), PDF (carousel), MP4 (Reel); YYYY-MM-DD_pillar_topic_v1.

8. Engagement analysis

  • Reel had the highest average reach (4,628 across 3 posts), while Single image + Story had the highest average ER (9.1%).
  • The best post by ER was Day 27: Decaf, done right (10.1%).
  • Behind the Roast led on saves per post (2 posts). With only 1–5 posts per pillar, treat this as a signal to test next month, not a conclusion.
  • The most link clicks came from Day 3: Light vs medium roast in 15s (30). Check in Insights whether those clicks came from the profile link or a Story sticker before shifting the mix. Offer posts should always carry a clear CTA.
  • Caveat: 15 posts is a small sample. Compare against the account's 90-day baseline and run at least 3 posts per format before changing the mix.

10. AI workflow and disclosure

  1. Ideation: an AI assistant suggested topic ideas per pillar. I selected 30 and checked them against the brand guardrails.
  2. Captions: posts marked "Draft by AI" were drafted from a prompt containing the voice guide and example captions, then edited by me for accuracy, voice and length. Posts marked "Human-written" were written without AI drafting.
  3. Fact check: brewing parameters use commonly published ranges and hedged wording. Origin and tasting claims are flagged for the roaster to confirm.
  4. Labels: AI use is shown per post in the calendar CSV and on the website.
